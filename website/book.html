5 minutes from the beach and Roses old town. Peace, quiet, and panoramic views.

Villa Victoria is a private home set inside a protected natural park above Roses, on the Costa Brava. Three bedrooms, panoramic sea views from every main room, and one unforgettable week with the whole family.

The villa is built for long family stays, not quick turnovers. Mornings on the terrace, afternoons in the sea, and the kind of quiet you only get inside a natural park — five minutes from town, but a world away.

The space. Private pool with infinity edge, jacuzzi, sauna, outdoor BBQ, fully equipped kitchen, home cinema, large terrace, and a garden that runs into the hillside. Sleeps 8 comfortably, up to 10 with the flexible sofa-bed setup.

Where you'll be

Roses, Girona, Spain

Villa Victoria sits on the mountain above Roses, inside a protected natural park. From the terrace you look out across the entire Gulf of Roses.

The beach is 5 minutes downhill by car, the old town and marina are 5 minutes away. Girona airport is 40 minutes; Barcelona is 1h 30. The Pyrenees and skiing are 2 hours north.
