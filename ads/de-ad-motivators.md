## Working hypotheses (replace with research)

The German market for Costa Brava holiday villas behaves differently from the UK on three axes worth probing in research:

1. **Driving distance, not just flying.** Germans drive to Spain in much higher numbers than Brits — the Roses → Munich/Stuttgart drive is a feature, not a footnote. This unlocks "longer stay, bigger luggage, bring the dog" angles.
2. **Family-quality bar is higher by default.** German families are accustomed to high-spec self-catering (Ferienhaus culture is mature). The "vs. shared hotel" pain doesn't land as hard. Compete on *destination + house quality + value*, not on "private vs. resort."
3. **Trust signals are different.** Germans want concrete numbers, certifications, energy efficiency, and clear cancellation terms upfront. The "knowledgeable local friend" voice still works but must be backed with specifics (kWh of solar, exact distances, water temp ranges).

## The Five Motivators (placeholder — refine with research)

### 1. Drivable Mediterranean — "Two days behind the wheel, two weeks under the Costa Brava sun."
**Hypothesis:** German families drive south for summer; Roses is reachable from Munich in ~12h. Hook the road-trip identity (Volvo XC90, dog in the boot, 2-week stay).
**Hook territory (DRAFT, not researched):**
- "Von München nach Roses: zwei Tagesetappen, dann 14 Tage Pool."
- "Die ganze Familie, der Hund, die Räder — alles passt rein. Kein Gepäcklimit am Flughafen."

### 2. Hochwertige Selbstversorgung — "A house that meets German standards."
**Hypothesis:** Direct comparison to Ferienhaus norms — induction kitchen, dishwasher, washer/dryer, real beds, fast Wi-Fi. Compete on spec, not on "private."
**Hook territory (DRAFT):**
- "Drei Bäder. Zwei Geschirrspüler. Echte Betten. So vermieten wir, wie wir selbst urlauben würden."

### 3. Cultural Pairing — "Dalí, Empordà, Catalan cuisine — all from one base."
**Hypothesis:** Germans index high on Bildungstourismus (cultural travel). Cadaqués + Dalí + Girona + Empordà food/wine is the angle.
**Hook territory (DRAFT):**
- "Cadaqués am Vormittag, Dalí-Museum am Nachmittag, Empordà-Wein zum Abend. Alles von einer Adresse."

### 4. Wetterflucht — "When Hamburg hits 14°C in May, you're already in the jacuzzi."
**Hypothesis:** Same weather-escape pull as the UK, but with a Spring/Autumn shoulder-season slant (Pfingsten, Herbstferien) — Germans book those windows aggressively.
**Hook territory (DRAFT):**
- "Pfingsten an der Costa Brava: 24°C, eigener Pool, kein Stau in der Toskana."

### 5. Group Value — "€650 pro Familie. Eigener Pool. Kein Hotelbuffet."
**Hypothesis:** Same friend-group math as the UK — likely lands in DE too. Translate, don't re-invent.

## Universal language guardrails

- **Use:** Concrete specs (m², kWh, km, °C), specific places, owner voice ("wir"), transparency.
- **Avoid:** "Exklusiv," "luxuriös," "VIP," "verwöhnen Sie sich" — same anti-status reflex as UK; if anything, German taste is more allergic to these.
