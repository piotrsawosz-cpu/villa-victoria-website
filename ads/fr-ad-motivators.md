## Working hypotheses (replace with research)

The French market for Costa Brava holiday villas has three dynamics worth probing:

1. **It's basically the next region down.** Roses is ~2h from the French border; Toulouse, Montpellier, Lyon are realistic weekend-and-week markets. The pitch is "still the Mediterranean, half the price of the Côte d'Azur."
2. **The Catalan crossover.** Northern Catalonia and southern France share food, language proximity, and the Pyrénées-Méditerranée identity. Lean into this — French audiences won't read Costa Brava as "abroad" the way Brits do.
3. **Cuisine and wine literacy is high.** Empordà wines, anchovies de l'Escala, Michelin presence (El Celler de Can Roca, Miramar) all land harder in FR than UK. The destination-credibility bar is met by food-and-wine name-dropping.

## The Five Motivators (placeholder — refine with research)

### 1. La Méditerranée Sans le Prix de la Côte — "Same sea. Half the price."
**Hypothesis:** Direct value-positioning vs. Côte d'Azur and Provence rentals. The math is brutal in our favour.
**Hook territory (DRAFT, not researched):**
- "La même Méditerranée que Saint-Trop'. À deux heures de la frontière. Sans le prix."

### 2. Frontière Floue — "Catalogne du Nord, Catalogne du Sud — c'est la même cuisine."
**Hypothesis:** The cultural-continuity angle. French audiences recognise Costa Brava as a Catalan extension of their own south, not as "Spain abroad."
**Hook territory (DRAFT):**
- "Anchois de l'Escala, vin de l'Empordà, et la mer juste là. Vous êtes à 2h de Perpignan."

### 3. Gastronomie — "El Celler de Can Roca à 90 minutes. Et chez vous, paella au feu de bois."
**Hypothesis:** Foodie destination-credibility. Empordà and Girona Michelin density + the home-kitchen + BBQ matters more to FR than UK.
**Hook territory (DRAFT):**
- "Trois étoiles à Girona. Marché de Roses le matin. Le BBQ de la villa le soir."

### 4. Espace Privé — "Votre piscine. Vos horaires. Pas de voisins de transat."
**Hypothesis:** Same Sovereign Space angle as UK #1; likely universal across markets — translate, don't re-invent.

### 5. Base pour Explorer — "Cadaqués, Cap de Creus, Dalí, Girona, Barcelone. Une seule adresse."
**Hypothesis:** Same Effortless Base angle as UK #5 — works in any market.

## Universal language guardrails

- **Use:** Specific gastronomy and place names (Empordà, l'Escala, Cadaqués, Cap de Creus), warm tutoiement-vouvoiement balance per the brand voice, owner voice ("nous").
- **Avoid:** "Luxueux," "exclusif," "VIP," "prestige" — French taste in this segment recoils from these as hard as the UK does. Specificity wins.
