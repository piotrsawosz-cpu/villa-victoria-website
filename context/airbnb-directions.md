## Getting to Villa Victoria

The closest airport is **Girona–Costa Brava (GRO)** — also known as Barcelona Girona — just about 40 minutes away by car. **Barcelona–El Prat (BCN)** is the larger alternative, around 1.5 to 2 hours from the villa, and usually a better option if you're flying long-haul or want more international routing.

**We strongly recommend renting a car.** Villa Victoria sits on a mountainside above Roses, inside a protected natural park, and a car is genuinely essential — both for the drive up and for making the most of the Costa Brava. The best beaches, restaurants, hiking trails, Girona's old town, and even day trips to Barcelona are all easiest by road. Rental desks are available at both Girona and Barcelona airports.

The final stretch up to the villa is a winding mountain road. It's beautiful — vineyards, sea views, glimpses of the natural park — but take it slowly, especially after dark. Your sat-nav or Google Maps will get you straight to the gate using the address on your booking.

**Public transport** is possible to Roses town (regular buses from Barcelona and Girona), but there's no service up to the villa itself. If you arrive without a car, you'll need a taxi or transfer for the final climb, and you'll be limited in what you can explore once you're here. Happy to recommend a local transfer service if it helps.

**Parking** is free and on-site, with space for multiple cars right at the villa. No street parking, no hunting for a spot.

## Check-In

Our local manager will meet you in person at the villa to hand over the keys and give you a proper walk-through — how the appliances work, where everything lives, the pool and sauna basics, and any local tips worth knowing. Think of it as a warm welcome rather than a formal check-in.

Once your trip is confirmed, just send us your estimated arrival time a day or two before you travel, so we can coordinate with the manager. If your flight is delayed or your plans shift en route, message us — we'll adjust.
