# Villa Victoria — Airbnb Pre-Booking Message

The short message guests must read and agree to **before they can request to book**. Purpose: welcome them warmly, set expectations on the key things, and filter out bad-fit bookings (events, mobility, pets, capacity). Each version verified under Airbnb's 500-character limit.

---

## 🇬🇧 English *(481 chars)*

Hi! Thanks for considering Villa Victoria for your trip. A few things to confirm before you book: this is our family's vacation home — we're not on site, but always reachable by message, and a trusted local contact handles check-in and check-out in person. Sleeps 8 comfortably (up to 10). No events or parties beyond your group. The villa is on a mountainside with stairs and a car is essential. Pets considered case by case — just ask. If that sounds good, we'd love to host you.
