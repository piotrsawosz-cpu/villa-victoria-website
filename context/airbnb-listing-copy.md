# Villa Victoria — Airbnb Listing Copy

Five drop-in sections for the Airbnb listing, written in Villa Victoria's voice: warm, welcoming, genuine, premium but never cold. Each section is trimmed to fit Airbnb's 500-character limit per field. Character counts noted under each heading.

## 1. Listing Description  *(451 chars)*

Villa Victoria is a beautiful three-bedroom home perched on the mountain above Roses, with panoramic views over the Gulf of Roses. Private pool, jacuzzi, sauna, BBQ terrace, and garden — set inside a natural park where the air is clean and the sunsets are unforgettable. The perfect base for families and groups: beaches and the old town are minutes away, Girona is 40 minutes by car, Barcelona an easy day trip. A real home, looked after with care.

## 2. The Space  *(498 chars)*

Villa Victoria sits high above Roses inside a protected natural park, with one of the most beautiful views on this stretch of coast — the whole Gulf of Roses opens up in front of you. Three bedrooms, three bathrooms, sleeps 8 comfortably (up to 10). Private pool, jacuzzi, wood-fired sauna, BBQ, garden, and a large terrace built for long lunches. Open living area, fully equipped kitchen, dedicated home cinema room. Peaceful and private — yet minutes from Roses, the beach, and hiking trails.

## 3. Guest Access  *(440 chars)*

You'll have the entire villa and grounds to yourselves — every bedroom, every bathroom, the pool, jacuzzi, sauna, BBQ, garden, terrace, cinema room, and kitchen. No shared spaces, no other guests on the property. Parking on-site. Wi-Fi covers the whole house and reaches the terrace. The home is fully equipped — linens, towels, kitchenware, BBQ tools — so you can settle in and feel at home from the moment you walk through the door.

## 4. Interaction with Guests  *(485 chars)*

Villa Victoria is our vacation home, so we're usually not on site. But we're always reachable by message — usually within an hour or two — and happy to help with restaurant recommendations, hidden coves, the best market days, or anything else that comes up. A trusted local contact handles check-in and check-out in person, gives you a proper walkthrough, and is on hand throughout your stay for anything practical. A friendly face when you arrive, a quick reply whenever you need one.

## 5. Other Things to Note  *(493 chars)*

A few things worth knowing: the villa sits on a mountainside, so winding roads on the way up — a car is essential. The property has multiple levels with outdoor stairs connecting the terraces, pool, and garden; not ideal for limited mobility, and small children should be supervised. Pool is unheated (best late spring to early autumn); sauna and jacuzzi run year-round. No events or parties beyond booked guest count. Pets considered case by case — just ask. Check-in 4pm, check-out 11am.
